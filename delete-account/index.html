Delete your Budvio account and data

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You can delete your Budvio account and the data we hold about you at any time, for free. This page explains how, what happens, and what we cannot delete.

Delete from inside the app

  • Open Budvio and tap Tools, then Settings, then Data & privacy.
  • Tap Export account data first if you want a copy of your data. It is free on every plan.
  • Tap Delete account, read what will happen and confirm.

You can cancel the deletion at any time during the 7-day cooling-off period by signing in and tapping Cancel deletion.

If you cannot open the app

Write to privacy@aigrowthmanager.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Budvio account". We will reply to confirm it is you, and then start the same process. If you signed in with Apple and chose "Hide My Email", write to us from the email address linked to your Apple account and tell us the private relay address shown in your Apple ID settings.

What happens next

  • Day 0. We record your request and send nothing to anyone. Your account stays usable during the cooling-off period.
  • Within 7 days. You can cancel. After the cooling-off period, deletion starts automatically.
  • After the cooling-off period. We permanently delete your account, your synced budget data, your receipt files, your devices and push tokens, and your link to any shared budgets. Messages you sent to support while signed in are deleted with your account.
  • Backups and logs. Copies in our backups and logs are removed on their normal schedule, no later than 30 days after deletion.

Shared budgets

If you own a shared budget, deleting your account deletes that budget for everyone in it, and the app warns you first so you can transfer or export what you need. Entries you added to someone else's budget are deleted with your account. Other people's own data is not affected.

What we cannot delete

  • Purchase records. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep records of subscriptions under their own policies and legal duties. Deleting your Budvio account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel in your store account too.
  • Information we must keep by law, and a short record that an account was deleted (with no budget content), kept for up to 12 months for security.

Data on your phone

Account deletion removes your data from our servers. The copy on your phone stays until you delete the app or use Tools, Reset data. If you use Budvio without an account, there is nothing on our servers to delete: delete the app or use Reset data, and your data is gone.
